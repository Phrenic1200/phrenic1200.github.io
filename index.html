About the Artist

Phrenic

Steven Leroy Morris, professionally known as Phrenic, is the founder, owner, recording artist and songwriter behind Schizophrenia Records LLC.

Rooted in Kansas City and the 816, Phrenic's creative world moves across hip-hop, R&B, inspirational storytelling, visual art and dark theatrical concepts.

The goal is simple: build an independent body of work with a recognizable identity, where music, artwork and story exist as one complete world.

“Create without permission. Tell the story honestly. Build the vision independently.”
